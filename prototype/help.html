FAQ
Is my health data private and secure?

Your data is locked up tighter than your feelings about that one aunt who asks questions about your dating life at Thanksgiving. It's encrypted in transit, stored on our servers, and never sold or shared. Only you can see it — not even we're nosy enough to look.

Can I export or back up my data?

Not yet! A shiny new data export feature is coming. Good news though — since your data now lives on our servers, clearing your browser history, cache, or cookies won't touch your logs. They'll be waiting for you next time you log in.

What happens to my data if I delete my account?

Deleting your account permanently removes all your logged data from our servers; for-e-ver. There's no undo button, no takesies backsies, no digital séance to summon it from the beyond.

How does the app identify patterns or triggers?

HashiTracker uses a bunch of magic (math and science) behind the scenes to play digital detective with your logged entries to see connections. Maybe your flareups have a suspicious habit of showing up after that third cup of coffee or a rough night's sleep. Case (potentially) solved!

What if I forget to log a day?

No guilt trips here! Life happens and the last thing you want is a scolding from us. Just pick back up when you can, missing a few days here and there won't break anything.

Can I edit or delete a past entry?

Not yet, but a future update will allow you to delete the evidence.

Do I have to fill out every section each day?

Nah, just enter what applies to you, whenever it applies.

What if a symptom or medication I need isn't listed?

The current list covers common symptoms and medications, but everyone's afflictions and daily concoctions are different. We're brewing up a future version, with fields for custom entry.

Can I use HashiTracker on multiple devices?

Yep! Log in on your phone, your laptop, your Playstation, even your smart fridge if you're really committed, and it'll all be right where you left it.

What if I forget my username or password?

No digital treasure hunt required. Just hit up the account recovery page, enter your email, and we'll send you a link to reset your password or remind you of your username.

Is this a substitute for medical advice?

Nope. HashiTracker did not go to medical school. It's more like a digital notebook, so you can go to your doctor with receipts instead of vibes.
