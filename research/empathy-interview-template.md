# Hashimoto User Research Interview

## Interview Questions

- When you have a flare-up, how do you try to "trace back" what caused it?
- What do you find frustrating about your current tracking method, or if you don't track your condition, why?
- What kind of data would you like to log? (e.g., stress, symptoms, meds, sleep, mood, diet, menstrual cycle, illness, exercise)
- How do you feel about getting reminders to track data?
- How do you feel about tracking your Hashimoto Disease? (Anxious, motivated, frustrated, empowered, overwhelmed, etc.)
- What would help you feel more supported or less isolated?
- What, if anything, would make it easy to use the app every day?
- What would deter you from using the app?
- How comfortable do you feel storing your health data in an app like this?
- Have you ever brought symptom or tracking data to a provider visit? If so, how did your provider respond to it?
- If you could tell the person designing this app one thing to make it truly helpful for people with Hashimoto's, what would you say?
